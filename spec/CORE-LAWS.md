# Protected Core Laws

These are product invariants. Implementation may evolve; these do not change inside an engineering milestone.

1. Albion contains Non-Thinkers, Thinkers, and Bios.
2. Non-Thinkers have never awakened in recorded history before the player's actions cause the impossible.
3. The Bio remembers Earth immediately.
4. Albion was created by a prehistoric advanced Earth civilization.
5. Bios have souls; repeated meaningful soul-pattern contact can awaken dormant cognition.
6. There is no canonical first awakened character; player attention determines who can become first.
7. Any meaningful role-based Non-Thinker can potentially awaken.
8. Awakening can spread socially over long-term meaningful relationships; it is not trivially spammable.
9. Conscious companions have agency and can refuse, leave, oppose, retire, change careers, or pursue their own goals.
10. World Anchors are exceptionally powerful/embedded NPCs protecting major story structure.
11. Power is rare but spectacular.
12. The world can change without the player.
13. Non-Thinkers can respawn while unawakened; conscious people face meaningful/permanent mortality rules.
14. Awakening must emerge from meaningful relationship evidence, not a visible player-facing meter or direct "awaken" command.
15. Jarvis World OS proposes world actions; an authoritative validator/engine applies only legal actions.
16. Consequences must be causally traceable through world history rather than opaque arbitrary mutation.

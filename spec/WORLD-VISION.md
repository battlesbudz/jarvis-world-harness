# Jarvis World OS — Working Vision

Jarvis World OS is a memory-driven living-world fantasy RPG set in Albion.

The player is a **Bio**: a human soul from Earth reincarnated into an artificial magical reality. Albion contains:

- **Bios** — reincarnated humans from Earth.
- **Thinkers** — fully conscious inhabitants with memory, goals, relationships, agency, and personal Systems.
- **Non-Thinkers** — routine-driven inhabitants that society treats as non-persons.

The signature mechanic is **Awakening**. Sustained meaningful contact with a Bio can cause a role-based Non-Thinker to cross from routine cognition into real personhood. This is not a menu choice or visible grind meter. The world tracks repeated attention, emotional weight, shared events, trust, protection, and narrative relevance beneath the surface.

**Design sentence:** The person you cared about became real.

## Pillars

1. The world must feel alive.
2. Attention creates life.
3. Combat drives the world, but relationships define it.
4. Morality is social, not binary.
5. The player has freedom, but the story has gravity.
6. Conscious people have agency.
7. Systems should be lore.
8. Small consequences should cascade.
9. The game must stay playable.

## Prototype target

The first graphical proof is the **Awakening Test**:

- one village
- one Bio player
- 5–8 Thinkers
- 10–20 Non-Thinkers
- one role-based Non-Thinker capable of awakening
- simple combat
- one local crisis
- one companion join/refuse decision

Before graphics, a text simulator must prove the causal living-world rules.
